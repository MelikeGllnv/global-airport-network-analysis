Global Airport Network Analysis

6,072 airports and 37,042 directed routes, analysed with three centrality measures to find the airports whose disruption would affect the network most. Hover over any airport on the maps to see its values.

Betweenness — bridge airports

Airports that lie on the most shortest paths between other airports.

Degree — connection density

Airports with the largest number of direct connections.

Closeness — accessibility

How quickly each airport can reach the rest of the network.
